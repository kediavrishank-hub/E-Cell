About Us
The Entrepreneurship Cell of Indian Institute of Management Calcutta, commonly known as ECell, is a student-run body that works towards promoting and encouraging the spirit of entrepreneurship in and around campus. ECell provides conducive environment for budding as well as aspiring entrepreneurs. ECell also provides its connections with ample opportunities by regularly organizing investor summits, startup workshops, seminars, business-plan competitions, guest talks and other similar events. We are a team of passionate individuals who are serious about entrepreneurship and want to make IIM Calcutta the start-up hub of eastern India.
Associated Institute bodies
IIM Calcutta Innovation Park (IIMCIP)
IIM Calcutta Innovation Park (IIMCIP) is a not-for-profit incubator under IIM Calcutta that has supported 300+ startups through mentoring, funding, and partnerships, driving innovation and sustainable growth.
IIM Calcutta Centre for Entrepreneurship and Innovation (IIM CEI)
IIM Calcutta Centre for Entrepreneurship and Innovation (IIM CEI) nurtures entrepreneurial talent among students and alumni through mentorship and collaborations, enabling them to build scalable, impactful ventures.
Genesis
Genesis
Every year ECell, IIM Calcutta organises The Entrepreneurship Summit- Genesis. It is one of the biggest entrepreneurship events in eastern India. The event brings together startup founders, industrialists, VCs, angel investors, and famous personalities from different domains, faculty and students to foster the spirit of entrepreneurship. This year we plan to go even bigger, and just like the name suggests, its the birth of something awesome!
Innovate | Automate | Accelerate
The theme for this year's Genesis, "Innovate. Automate. Accelerate: The New Age of AI and Investment in Indian Startups," highlights a critical shift in the startup world: Artificial Intelligence is no longer just a feature, but a fundamental driver of business growth.
For entrepreneurs, this is a call to action to leverage AI for deep automation, enabling them to build faster, leaner, and more scalable companies. For investors, the theme underscores the opportunity to back ventures that use AI to create a lasting competitive advantage — an 'innovation moat', with proprietary data and efficient models.
Genesis is the forum where these forces connect. It brings together IIM Calcutta's entrepreneurial ecosystem with the nation's promising AI innovators and strategic investors, all focused on driving the next chapter of India's startup journey.
